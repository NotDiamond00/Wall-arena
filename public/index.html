WALL ARENA

Block your rivals. Reach the far edge first.

Room

    How to play

    Goal: bring your token to the opposite edge (blue ↑, red ↓, green →, yellow ←). Your goal edge is tinted in your colour.

    Your turn: move one step (tap a dot or use the arrow keys) or place one wall. Walls are 2 cells long. Pick Wall ▬ or ▮, tap a grid corner to preview, then Place wall. Walls can’t overlap or cross, and can’t cut anyone off completely.

    Jumps: an adjacent token can be jumped; if a wall or the edge is behind it, step diagonally around it.

    Walls: every player gets ⌊size² ÷ 10⌋: 10 on 10×10 up to 250 on 50×50.

    Winning: 1v1 and free-for-all: first to their goal. 1v2 / 1v3: blue plays alone and wins by reaching the goal; the other team wins when any teammate does. Blue moves first, then seats rotate.

    Timer: a missed turn is played for you (a step toward the goal); 3 misses in a row forfeit. Offline players are auto-played and can reconnect with the same browser.
